# Risk register

Status: plan for review. Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are High / Medium / Low. "Owner area" is
the label of the group that tracks the risk. Evidence references (V…) point to
`docs/plan/verification.md`; question references (Q…) to `docs/plan/open-questions.md`.

## 1. Top five

1. **R2 — Scope against capacity.** Twelve domains, five robot classes, a renderer, a viewer and
   a packaging story are years of work for a small team.
2. **R1 — OpenUSD build and distribution.** Not packaged for Ubuntu 26.04; untested with
   GCC 15, CMake 4 and Python 3.14; may not fit the ROS buildfarm.
3. **R3 — Co-simulation coupling stability.** The electrical ↔ mechanical loop can be unstable or
   inaccurate with naive exchange, and it is the core of the wedge.
4. **R7/R9 — Upstream stacks not on Lyrical.** Autoware has no Lyrical release; PX4 and ArduPilot
   document Jazzy and Humble.
5. **R12 — Realism claims without data.** Sensor, weather, EMI and battery models need reference
   data that the project does not yet have.
