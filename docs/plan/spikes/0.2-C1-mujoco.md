# Spike 0.2-C1: MuJoCo source and validation

Date: 2026-10-14. Questions (roadmap issue 0.2-C1, open question Q9): where does the MuJoCo
backend get MuJoCo from, and does MuJoCo meet the validation table of
`docs/design/domains/rigid_body.md` §7 at the 1 ms step?

**Recommendation:** use `ros-lyrical-mujoco-vendor` (MuJoCo 3.12.0) through rosdep key
`mujoco_vendor`, and run the backend with `cone="elliptic"`. The validation suite now lives in
`modules/rigid_body/mujoco_backend/test/` and runs in CI.

## Sources compared

| Source | MuJoCo | Contents | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ros-lyrical-mujoco-vendor` 0.1.0 (ROS apt, built 2026-08-26) | 3.12.0 (`mjVERSION_HEADER 3012000`) | `libmujoco.so.3.12.0`, headers, `simulate` as a static library, sample models; installs under `/opt/ros/lyrical/opt/mujoco_vendor` | CMake: `find_package(mujoco_vendor)` gives target `mujoco::mujoco`. | `libmujoco-dev` (Ubuntu apt) | 3.5.0 | Library and CMake config | Seven minor versions older |

Both were inspected by downloading the `.deb` files (`apt-get download`) and unpacking them, not by
installing. The vendor package is what `mujoco_ros2_control` in Lyrical uses, so a comparison
with it (roadmap F3) runs on the same MuJoCo.

## Validation results (MuJoCo 3.12.0, step 1 ms)

| rigid_body.md §7 case | Scene | Result | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free fall | sphere with a free joint, 10 s | RK4: exact to 1e-6 m. Euler: equals the semi-implicit closed form `-g dt² n(n+1)/2`, i.e. 4.9 cm behind the true parabola after 10 s | — |
| Pendulum energy drift | 1 kg point mass on 1 m, amplitude 1 rad, 10 s | RK4 1.3e-13 of m·g·L; Euler, implicit and implicitfast 6.8e-4 (identical: without damping the implicit integrators reduce to semi-implicit Euler) | < 1e-3 |
| Large-angle period | same | RK4 period within 5.3e-10 of `4·sqrt(L/g)·K(sin(θ0/2))`; the remainder is the 1e-9 kg·m² rotational inertia MuJoCo requires | 1e-5 (test) |
| Double pendulum energy drift | two 1 kg point masses, 10 s | RK4 1.0e-11 of m·g·L | < 1e-3 |
| Sliding block, stick/slip angle | box on a plane, μ = 0.5, gravity tilted in 0.1° steps | **elliptic cone: 26.6°**; **pyramidal cone (MuJoCo's default): 25.1°**. Coulomb: 26.565° | ± 1° |
| `effective_inertia` | planar two-link arm, q2 = 0.7 rad | `mj_fullM` equals the analytic joint-space inertia matrix within 1e-9 relative | 1e-9 |
| Determinism | box and capsule falling and colliding, 5 s, run twice in one process | bit-identical `qpos` and `qvel` | identical |

## Consequences for the backend (0.2-C2)

1. **Friction cone:** the pyramidal default slides 1.5° early, outside the tolerance. The
   backend sets `cone="elliptic"`. A test (`Incline.PyramidalConeSlipsEarly`) records the
   pyramidal behaviour so an upgrade that changes it is noticed.
2. **Integrator:** the default semi-implicit Euler meets the energy tolerance (6.8e-4 < 1e-3) but
   lags free fall by half a step. `implicitfast` is the usual choice for robots with joint
   damping and is what the backend should default to; RK4 is available for validation runs. The
   profile's `fidelity` field can select it later. TODO: measure the cost of each on the AMR.
3. **Point masses** need a small positive inertia in MuJoCo; importers must supply physically
   valid inertia (MuJoCo refuses tensors that violate the triangle inequality, e.g.
   `diaginertia="0.01 0.01 0.05"`, unless `balanceinertia` is set). The URDF importer should
   report such links rather than silently balancing them.
