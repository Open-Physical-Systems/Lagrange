# Lagrange v0.3.0: arms, electrical and thermal (release notes, draft)

v0.2 was the simulation core and the AMR. v0.3 adds the electrical and thermal domains, a robot
arm with MoveIt 2, a raster camera, a second physics engine, and replay in the C++ server. The
AMR now drives on its own power system: a battery, a DC bus and two motor drives at 100 µs.

**Status:** prepared, not published. Merging `dev` into `main` and tagging stay with the owner.

## Highlights

- **Electrical.**
  - Six USD schemas (`ElectricalMotorAPI_1`, `BatteryAPI_1`, `PortAPI_1`, bus, load,
    converter), with ports on the same prims as the 3D parts.
  - A network module: DC bus with capacitor, battery equivalent circuit, averaged dq motor
    drives with current loops, regeneration, brake resistor and trips, DC-DC converters
    between buses.
  - Controllers command torque; the electrical domain delivers it, or its ideal substitute does.
  - Overlay files give an imported robot motors, a battery and a bus without editing its URDF.
  - Export to an ngspice netlist or a Modelica model (`lagrange export`).
  - An FMI 3.0 co-simulation host, with a reference FMU (`lagrange_models/dc_drive`).
- **Thermal.**
  - Lumped thermal networks (`ThermalAPI_1`, `ThermalLinkAPI_1`) heated by the electrical
    losses, with derating.
  - The network runs when the site's ambient temperature is given.
- **Coupling.** Stability checks across the electrical, thermal and physics rates at start-up;
  physics reports each joint's effective inertia for the motor models.
- **Bench mode.** `--bench run.mcap` replaces physics by a recorded run; electrical, thermal and
  sensors run live, at 10 kHz electrical, about 59× real time.
- **Replay.** `lagrange_server --replay run.mcap` replays a recording to the viewer, with
  seeking. The Python mock server is now a test tool (Q15).
- **Arm.** The sample 6-DoF arm `arm6` in a workcell; MoveIt 2 pick-and-place through
  `joint_trajectory_controller` at 1 kHz; a wrist camera.
- **Camera.** A raster camera sensor (colour, depth, instance ids) on the same mesh pipeline as
  the viewer's viewport, published as ROS `Image` and `CameraInfo` and shown in the viewer.
- **Physics.**
  - Jolt Physics 5.6 as a complete second backend (`--physics jolt`, or the profile's
    `rigid_body.backend`), checked against MuJoCo by an agreement suite.
  - Convex hull colliders in the world, in USD, in both backends and in the lidar (CPU and
    GPU).
  - `collision_bake` makes them from render meshes with CoACD.
- **Schemas and profiles.** `migrate_schema` and `migrate_profile` bring old files up to date;
  the stage loader names the command when it meets an older schema version.
- **Viewer.**
  - Scene editing: schema values change in a running simulation from the inspector.
  - The Electrical workspace runs on the simulator's power system.
  - Thermal false-colour and unlit shading in the viewport.
  - The camera view in the Sensors workspace.

## Known issues

- **Jolt: drive wheels slip while ramping up.** On Jolt's rigid contacts the sample AMR's
  casters take most of the load; after the 6 s demo drive Jolt is about 0.4 m behind MuJoCo.
  MuJoCo stays the default.
- **Nav2 start-up** (from v0.2): some launches have one ROS process never discover another's
  endpoint. Mitigated by retries; root cause not found.
- **Not run while GitHub Actions are disabled:** the sanitizer builds of the USD packages and
  the nightly jobs. Everything else was verified locally with colcon.
