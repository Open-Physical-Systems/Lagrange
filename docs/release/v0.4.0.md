# Lagrange v0.4.0: drones (release notes)

v0.3 added arms and the electrical and thermal domains. v0.4 adds drones: rotors built from
their propellers' blades, wind and turbulence, GNSS, magnetometer and barometer, a sample
quadcopter that flies on its own power system, and PX4 and ArduPilot flying it in lockstep.

**Status:** released on 2026-10-25 as `v0.4.0`, at the owner's request.

## Highlights

- **Rotors.**
  - `RotorAPI_1` on rotor joints, with the propeller's hand, its mount (puller or pusher) and
    its propeller file (`*.prop.yaml`: blade chord, twist and sections from root to tip).
  - The `coefficients` fidelity: thrust and torque from measured CT and CP, inflow lag,
    Cheeseman–Bennett ground effect.
  - The `bem` fidelity: blade-element momentum on the propeller's geometry, in climb, descent,
    forward flight and wind; turning the wrong way or mounted back to front, a propeller gives
    what a real one gives. Checked against NACA Report 640's full-scale propeller, with nothing
    fitted. Each propeller is tabulated once at load, so a step is a lookup.
  - Folding blades that hang folded when stopped and open as they spin up.
  - Rotors are drawn as their blades in the viewer, spinning and folding.
- **Aerodynamics and wind.** Link drag (`AeroAPI_1`) against a logarithmic mean wind with
  Dryden turbulence; the wind reaches the rotors (`env/wind/velocity`).
- **Sensors.** GNSS, magnetometer (World Magnetic Model 2025) and barometer (ICAO standard
  atmosphere) on the site's geo origin, published on ROS 2 as `NavSatFix`, `MagneticField`,
  `FluidPressure` and `Temperature`.
- **Autopilots.** PX4 v1.17.0 SITL through its simulator MAVLink interface, and ArduPilot
  Copter-4.7.2 through its JSON interface, both in lockstep (`--mavlink px4|ardupilot`). Both fly
  a square mission, calm and in 8 m/s of gusty wind.
- **Electrical.** Small fast motors at 50 µs; a low battery sags the bus and costs thrust.
- **Samples.** `quad_x500` (URDF, overlay, USD) with generic 10-inch propellers matched to the
  measured APC 10x4.7 SF; variants with folding blades and with motors under the arms
  (pushers); two- and three-blade propellers; the `open_field` world; profiles for hover, gusty
  wind and the blade-element fidelity.
- **Docs.** The drone tutorial, from the quadcopter's files to a PX4 mission
  (`docs/guide/tutorial-drone.md`, run by `tools/ci/tutorial_drone.sh`).

## Known issues

- **Blade-element limits.** No wake skew or blade flapping beyond the hub moment; no stall
  delay, so a highly pitched propeller's thrust near static is under-predicted
  (aerodynamics.md §7.1).
- **Dryden turbulence.** MIL-HDBK-1797 equation numbers are marked TODO(verify) in the code.
- **Viewer.** Rotors at hundreds of rad/s are drawn at the frame rate, so the blades strobe.
- **Start-up warnings.** `lagrange validate` and the Python API build a run before they apply a
  profile, so they log that rotors and geo sensors need their site parameters even when the
  profile gives them.
- From v0.3: Jolt's drive-wheel slip, Nav2's occasional start-up discovery failure.
- **Not run while GitHub Actions are disabled:** the sanitizer builds and the nightly jobs.
  Everything else was verified locally with colcon.
