# Lagrange v0.5.0: weather, interference and radio links (release notes)

v0.4 added drones. v0.5 adds the world around the robot:
- the site's atmosphere, with fog, rain and dust that blind lidars and cameras and wet the floor;
- volumetric fields of fog and wind, including wind imported from OpenFOAM;
- electromagnetic interference from power lines, drives, welders and transmitters;
- radio links and CAN buses whose losses reach the robot's ROS 2 topics.

**Status:** not released. Drafted on 2026-10-25 and waiting for the owner's decision.

## Highlights

- **Weather** (`lagrange_weather`, domains/weather.md §8).
  - The site has no defaults. Temperature, pressure and humidity come from the profile,
    `--weather` or a `WeatherAPI_1` prim.
  - Moist air density.
  - Fog: Koschmieder at 550 nm, Kim's wavelength exponent for 905 and 1550 nm.
  - Rain: Marshall–Palmer. Dust: quartz spheres.
  - Behavioural wet friction (applied by both physics backends) and lens contamination.
  - The `clear` substitute.
- **Sensors in weather.**
  - The lidar's range follows the lidar equation through the extinction and a dirty window.
  - False returns from fog backscatter and from raindrops, on their own random stream.
  - The camera's Koschmieder veil.
  - All per beam on the CPU, on every tier.
- **Fields** (`lagrange_fields`, NanoVDB 10.0.1, MPL-2.0).
  - Fog and wind grids, compressed to a few kB.
  - The lidar integrates fog along each beam, the camera per block of pixels, and link drag and
    rotors fly in the wind grid.
  - The viewer's Environment workspace gets the real weather and slices of fog, wind and |B|.
  - Samples: a fog bank in the warehouse and a sheared wind with a wake over the open field.
- **Wind from CFD.** `lagrange_foam_to_vdb` turns an OpenFOAM case's ASCII cell centres and
  velocities into a wind grid. OpenFOAM is not a dependency.
- **EMI** (`lagrange_emi`, domains/emi.md §8).
  - Sources:
    - power lines, as straight conductors with harmonics;
    - motor drives, as dipoles driven by their motor's current;
    - welders;
    - transmitters, by their far field;
    - field maps.
  - Victims: magnetometers add the field at their own rate, so a 60 Hz line aliases, and IMUs
    add noise.
  - Encoder glitches (applied to ros2_control's encoders), CAN bit-error rate, radio
    interference power and harness voltage.
  - Grid frequencies are site parameters. The `zero` substitute.
- **Comms** (`lagrange_comms`, domains/comms.md §8).
  - Radio links: log-distance path loss, walls of the fixed scenery, correlated shadowing, noise
    plus EMI interference, AWGN bit errors for BPSK to 64-QAM, and retries.
  - CAN fault confinement by ISO 11898-1.
  - `--link-topics` makes the ROS 2 bridge lose and delay mapped topics, `/cmd_vel` included.
  - The `ideal` substitute.
- **Schemas.**
  - New: `WeatherAPI_1`, `EmiSourceAPI_1`, `EmiSusceptibilityAPI_1`, `SensorMaterialAPI_1`,
    `RadioLinkAPI_1`, `CanBusAPI_1`.
  - The loader places every prim on its body, so sources, victims and radios move with the
    robot.
- **Command line.** `--weather`, `--link-topics`, and `--domain weather|emi|comms=off`. Units
  `s` and `bit/s`.
- **Samples.**
  - Profiles: `amr_fog.yaml`, `quad_x500_power_line.yaml`.
  - Robots: `quad_x500_emi.usda`, `amr_diffdrive_wifi.usda` with its link map.
  - World: `power_line.usda`.
  - Fields: `assets/fields/*.nvdb`.
- **Docs.** The environment tutorial (`docs/guide/tutorial-environment.md`, run by
  `tools/ci/tutorial_environment.sh`).

## Known issues

- **Not applied yet:** CAN errors on any bus traffic but the modelled node's, and the MAVLink
  bridge's telemetry over a link.
  `SensorMaterialAPI_1` is defined but no sensor reads it.
- **Uncalibrated:** the lidar's fog clutter gain, and the indoor radio parameters of the samples.
- **NanoVDB** is header-only (`libnanovdb-dev`). Until it is installed on a machine, the build
  uses a local copy of the headers (`NANOVDB_INCLUDE_DIR` or `~/.local/nanovdb`).
- **Start-up warnings.** The Python API and `lagrange validate` build a run before they apply a
  profile, so they still log the missing site parameters that the profile then gives.
- From earlier releases: Jolt's drive-wheel slip, Nav2's occasional start-up discovery failure,
  rotor strobing in the viewer.
- **Not run while GitHub Actions are disabled:** the sanitizer builds and the nightly jobs.
  Everything else was verified locally with colcon: 807 tests above `lagrange_core`, 0
  failures.
