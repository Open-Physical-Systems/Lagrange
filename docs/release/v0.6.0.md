# Lagrange v0.6.0: cars and autonomous cars (release notes)

v0.5 added the world around the robot. v0.6 adds road vehicles and the world they drive in:
- cars with tyres, suspension, brakes, steering and a powertrain;
- road networks from OpenDRIVE, with traffic and pedestrians on them;
- OpenSCENARIO scenarios;
- the sensors of an automated car: radar and a camera sensor model;
- interfaces for autonomy stacks, Autoware's included.

**Status:** draft. Not released: `dev` is not merged into `main` and nothing is tagged. That
waits for the owner's request.

## Highlights

- **Vehicle dynamics** (`lagrange_vehicle_dynamics`, domains/vehicle_dynamics.md §8).
  - A raycast vehicle on top of either physics backend, at 1 ms.
  - Suspension preloaded at the ride height, with anti-roll bars.
  - MF-Tyre 5.2 tyres from `.tir` files (pure and combined slip), or linear tyres, with
    relaxation lengths.
  - Brakes with bias and ABS. Ackermann steering with a rate limit and lag.
  - Electric or engine-map powertrains with an automatic gearbox.
  - The `ideal` substitute, a kinematic bicycle.
- **Road networks** (`lagrange_opendrive`, formats.md §3.11).
  - Its own OpenDRIVE reader: lines, arcs, spirals, cubic polynomials, elevation, lane sections
    and offsets, marks, links and junctions.
  - `lagrange_opendrive_import` writes road surfaces, road marks and `RoadLaneAPI_1` lanes.
- **Traffic** (`lagrange_traffic`, domains/traffic.md §8).
  - Kinematic agents, moved by MuJoCo as mocap bodies and by Jolt as kinematic bodies.
  - IDM cars on the lanes, fixed-cycle lights, first-come right of way at junctions.
  - Social-force pedestrians on the sidewalks.
  - `--traffic vehicles=N,pedestrians=M,seed=S`; the `zero` substitute.
- **Scenarios** (`lagrange_openscenario`, traffic.md §8.1).
  - Its own runner for a subset of OpenSCENARIO 1.x: parameters, entities, Init, speed and
    lane-change actions, and time, position, distance, speed and travelled conditions.
  - `--scenario FILE.xosc`: the scenario's roads are the world unless `--world` is given.
- **Sensors** (domains/sensors.md §2).
  - Radar by ray launching (`RadarAPI_1`): echoes from the bodies' radar cross-section density,
    one detection per object and range cell, Doppler, SNR by the radar equation.
  - The camera's sensor model: lens distortion (plumb_bob, equidistant), vignetting, a
    rotational rolling shutter, exposure with shot and read noise, and auto exposure.
    `camera_info` carries the lens.
- **Driving a car over ROS 2.** `/ackermann_cmd` (steering angle and speed) for any road
  vehicle, beside `/cmd_vel`.
- **Autoware** (`lagrange_autoware`, docs/guide/autoware.md).
  - A vehicle interface: Autoware's control, gear and indicator commands and its control-mode
    request in; velocity, steering, gear and control-mode reports and a ground-truth kinematic
    state out.
  - It builds only when the Autoware messages are found, since `autoware_vehicle_msgs` has no
    Lyrical binary.
- **Schemas.**
  - New libraries: `vehicle` (`VehicleAPI_1`, `WheelAPI_1`) and `road` (`RoadLaneAPI_1`).
  - New: `RadarAPI_1`.
  - `CameraSensorAPI_1` gains `electronRate` and `vignetting`.
  - Robots are named by their top prim, and kinematic bodies are part of the world tables.
- **Samples.**
  - The sedan: a URDF, with a USD layer for its vehicle schemas, radar and camera model.
  - The city block: buildings, and OpenDRIVE roads written by `city_block_xodr.py`.
  - The tyre file `generic_205_55R16.tir`, the scenario `cut_in.xosc`, and the profile
    `sedan_city.yaml`.
- **Docs.**
  - The cars tutorial (`docs/guide/tutorial-cars.md`, run by `tools/ci/tutorial_cars.sh`).
  - The Autoware guide.

## Known issues

- **Not built, by the owner's v0.6 decision:** the PhysX backend, SUMO co-simulation and esmini.
- **Traffic:** the lights are not drawn, pedestrians do not cross roads, no lane changes or
  priority rules.
- **Radar:** no rain attenuation, multipath or ghosts. Ground-plane echoes are dropped as
  clutter.
- **Camera:** no motion blur, no absolute light level (the scene's brightness is the camera's
  `electronRate`), no colour filter array. SDFormat's `<distortion>` and `<noise>` are not
  imported.
- **Autoware:** the `/sensing/...` remaps in the launch file follow Autoware's sample sensor kit
  and need checking against the kit in use.
- **Scenarios:** editing the scene after a scenario has ended logs its end a second time.
- From earlier releases: Jolt's drive-wheel slip, Nav2's occasional start-up discovery failure,
  rotor strobing in the viewer.
- **Not run while GitHub Actions are disabled:** the sanitizer builds and the nightly jobs.
  Everything else was verified locally with colcon: 882 tests above `lagrange_core` (with the
  Autoware messages underlay), 0 failures.
