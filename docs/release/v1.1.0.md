# Lagrange v1.1.0: easier to use, checked against measurements (release notes)

v1.1 is the first feature release on the stable 1.x line. It makes Lagrange easier to start and
to drive: one `lagrange` command, one run file, robots added while it runs. It adds plugins,
walking people, real robot models, water, a wind tunnel and rotor downwash, a realistic viewport,
and many new sensors. Several models are now compared with measured data. The documentation is
published as a site with a physics reference for every model.

Everything frozen by RFC 0007 changed only by addition. The plugin ABI went from 1.0 to 1.1 by
appending, and new schemas were added (`CustomSensorAPI_1`, `HumanAPI_1`, `WaterAPI_1`,
`ThermalCameraAPI_1`, `JointDynamicsAPI_1`, `ElectricalBranchAPI_1` and others), all checked by
the frozen-surface tests. v1.0 files, plugins, profiles and recordings still load.

**Status:** released on 2026-11-07 as `v1.1.0`, on the owner's request. Documentation:
<https://open-physical-systems.github.io/Lagrange/>.

## Highlights

### Easier to run

- **`lagrange`** runs the server and the viewer together, the way `gazebo` does
  (`lagrange amr`, `lagrange quad`, `lagrange sedan`, `lagrange swerve`, `lagrange town`, or
  `lagrange ROBOT [WORLD]`). It also runs a profile with the viewer (`lagrange PROFILE.yaml`).
- **The run file** (`docs/guide/run-file.md`) holds a whole run in one YAML file: robot, world,
  what to simulate and the site. Your own robot starts still on an empty ground.
- **Spawning.** Start without a robot, then add and remove robots and people while it runs: with
  `lagrange spawn` and `lagrange delete`, File → Add robot in the viewer, or ROS 2
  `simulation_interfaces`.
- **Joint control.** Any robot can be driven joint by joint with no controller, over
  `/joint_commands`.
- **Site changes while running.** `lagrange set gravity=1.62 wind_speed=5` and the viewer's
  Environment panel change the site as it runs: gravity, wind, temperature, rain, fog, pressure
  and humidity. The panel also has weather presets.
- **`lagrange check`** says which joints cannot hold the robot. **`lagrange bake`** splits a
  URDF's concave collision meshes into convex parts.
- **Examples** (`examples/`): one ROS 2 package per use, each run by a launch file. They cover
  your own robot, an arm, a power system, a lidar, fog, the quadcopter, the Holybro X500 V2 by
  teleop, a car, spawning, ros2_control, a C plugin, learning, a trotting quadruped, a camera and
  the wind tunnel.
- **Python.** `lagrange.envs` offers Gymnasium's interface over a simulation, with environments
  running in parallel.
- **A TX16S-style remote on screen** (`utils/lagrange_tx16s`). Fly it from a real RadioMaster
  TX16S over USB or from the keyboard.

### Plugins and people

- **Plugins in the server** (RFC 0008). Plugins are loaded with `--plugin`, through plugin ABI
  1.1. A plugin can:
  - see the world and the scene (rays, overlaps);
  - simulate custom sensors (the sample `lagrange_tof` time-of-flight and ultrasonic sensor);
  - map signals to and from ROS topics (`--ros-topics`);
  - replace the physics engine, which a conformance suite then checks.

  Start from the plugin template.
- **Walking humans** (RFC 0009). People are 14 kinematic capsule segments. They walk with their
  stance foot planted and steer round robots and each other. Add them as crowds (`--humans N`),
  one by one (`lagrange spawn`) or as SDFormat actors. A thermal camera sees them at skin
  temperature.

### Robots and worlds

- Real models, fetched with checksums (`fetch_assets`):
  - the Lexus RX 450h (Autoware's sample vehicle);
  - the Holybro X500 V2 (PX4's frame);
  - the AMR_FMS swerve AMR in its 60 m × 40 m warehouse.
- Real places built from open data, Zurich and Nishi-Shinjuku in Tokyo.
- **The town.** Working lights, zebra crossings whose buttons people press, vans and buses,
  lane changes, and four seasons with snow and ice.
- **Water** (`WaterAPI_1`, `--water`): buoyancy, drag, added mass, currents and linear waves.
  Fossen's model takes a vehicle's identified coefficients, and the BlueROV2 Heavy's are
  included.
- A trotting quadruped example.

### Physics

- **Rotor downwash.** The jet under a drone, its outwash on the ground, and the wake against walls
  and roofs, felt by other robots and checked against OpenFOAM hover cases. A **live
  lattice Boltzmann air box** can run round the drone on the GPU (`downwash_model: lbm`), and
  the wind blows through it.
- Rotors: the vortex-ring state from NeuroBEM's fit, the ceiling effect, and the air's torque
  turning the propeller so a multirotor yaws.
- **The wind tunnel** (`lagrange tunnel`): lattice Boltzmann on the collision shapes, on the
  GPU or the deterministic CPU reference, with a grid study. **`lagrange foam`** writes and runs
  the same case in OpenFOAM.
- **The weather reaches the drone.** Rotors, drag and the controller fly in the weather's air
  density, and rain and snow push links.
- Electrical:
  - velocity and voltage motor modes, and motors on sliding joints;
  - a SPICE netlist or a URDF `<lagrange>` block as the power system;
  - resistors, fuses, switches and diodes between buses (RFC 0010).
- Rigid body:
  - joint damping, friction and mimic joints from every robot format;
  - each body's contact force, and a force-torque reading at each joint, in MuJoCo and Jolt;
  - mesh colliders as convex hulls;
  - gravity changed while it runs.

### Sensors

- Lidar intensity from surface materials, incidence angle and range.
- Lidar motion distortion on both tiers.
- Sensor latency and jitter, applied the same way for ROS, plugins, recordings, Python and the
  viewer.
- A thermal camera (`ThermalCameraAPI_1`): Planck band radiance, with a Swinbank clear sky.
- Each camera's instance image on ROS 2.
- Camera sensors drawn with the viewport's realistic renderer.

### The viewer

- **Realistic shading**, the default on a GPU: sky, sun shadows, physically based materials, real
  water, photo textures, and falling rain and snow.
- The Electrical workspace gains Circuit, Netlist and 3D overlay tabs, and Auto-layout.
- A battery's State cards and OCV plot.
- The Signals list gains a unit filter and drag onto the scope.
- The Environment panel gains live site entry and weather presets.
- An angle flight mode for multicopters. Airspeed and tilt are shown beside the ground speed.

### Checked against measurements

`tools/ci/realism.sh` runs these checks (`docs/validation/report.md`):

| Check | Reference | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | LG HG2 cell (Kollmeyer et al.): pulses and drive cycles | 5.4–6.2 mV RMS from 46 to 89 % SoC. 21.5 mV at 24 % misses the 20 mV target. Unseen LA92 11.7 mV, US06 20.1 mV (just over). No temperature dependence |
| Planar pushing | MCube (Yu et al. 2016) | MuJoCo within 6 mm and 4–7° of the quasi-static model. **Jolt does not turn the block** |
| Rotors in flight | NeuroBEM flights (Bauersfeld et al. 2021) | `coefficients` 1.46 N RMS (their fit 1.49). `bem` 1.12 N (their BEM 0.98) |
| Drone under a drone | Kiran et al. 2025 | 76 % of thrust kept at 4 l against 65 % measured (**11 points over**) |
| Underwater vehicle | BlueROV2 Heavy (von Benzon et al. 2022) | Top speeds 0.729 / 0.626 / 0.713 m/s against 0.72 / 0.63 / 0.71 |
| Wind tunnel | Ahmed body (SAE 840300) | **Over four times too much drag**. Wall friction 3.1 times too high |

### Documentation

- **A documentation site** (Sphinx, published to GitHub Pages from `main`). It has diagrams,
  videos and highlighted code.
- An installation guide, from ROS 2 Lyrical to a first run.
- **A physics reference for every model:** what it computes, its equations, how it is solved,
  and how far to trust it.
- A **Robots and worlds** page showing each sample running, with its command.
- New tutorials: your own drone step by step, a power system from a schematic, people around
  your robot. There are also feature videos of real runs.

## Known issues

- The misses in the table above, and everything listed as owed in the validation report. There is
  still no sim-vs-real recording of a real robot (the owner has no hardware to record).
- Not run while GitHub Actions are disabled: the sanitizer builds and the nightly jobs. The
  release was checked by a local `colcon build` and `colcon test --packages-above lagrange_core`.
- Radiation stays a zero substitute.
