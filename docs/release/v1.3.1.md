# Lagrange v1.3.1: the home page and a measured comparison (release notes)

v1.3.1 changes only the documentation site. The simulator, its packages and their behaviour are
those of v1.3.0; nothing needs to be reinstalled.

**Status:** draft. It is released with the simulator comparison, once its measurements are done.

## Documentation

- **The home page opens on what Lagrange does.** A wall of nine running clips and pictures (live
  air on the GPU, wakes on walls, weather on the sensors, lidar returns by material, heat inside
  the parts, a robot's own interference, 40 km worlds, traffic and people, cars with real
  tyres), what is new in 1.3, and four numbers with their sources: 9 physics domains, 45
  sensors, 12.9 times real time for the sedan in the city block (headless, a shared laptop,
  ±30 %; `docs/design/benchmarks.md`), 1 799 tests.
- **A comparison with other simulators**, measured. TODO: the comparison page's summary, once
  its measurements are in (`docs/comparison.md`).
- The sensor count is 37 built-in kinds (the imaging radar was missing from the count).
