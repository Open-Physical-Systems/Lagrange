# Lagrange v1.5.0: actuators, lamps, fire, and trees a drone can fly into (release notes)

v1.5.0 is a feature release on the stable 1.x line. The schemas, plugin ABI, protocol, profiles
and recordings changed only by addition (RFC 0007). The behaviour changes are listed under
"Upgrading from 1.4.x" below.

**Status:** released on 2026-11-22 as `v1.5.0`, on the owner's request. Documentation:
<https://open-physical-systems.github.io/Lagrange/>.

## Install

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Open-Physical-Systems/Lagrange/main/packaging/install.sh | bash
source /opt/ros/lyrical/setup.bash
lagrange amr
```

## Actuators (RFC 0020)

- **Grippers:** suction cups that seal, leak and pull with `Δp·A`, and magnets whose pull is
  `B²A/μ0`. Both hold parts by force and friction, never by a weld.
- **Lead screws:** back-drive efficiency, so a self-locking screw holds its load with the motor
  off.
- **Servos:** hobby and smart servos, with the board's pulse map, PID and overload shutdown.
- **Steppers:** steppers on a chopper driver that lose steps under overload, as real ones do.
- **Solenoids:** solenoids from an inductance table.
- **Brakes:** spring-applied holding brakes released by a coil.
- **Thrusters:** marine thrusters from an open-water chart.
- **ros2_control:** GPIO interfaces bound to all of the above.
- **Samples:** a suction arm, a screw lift, a pan-tilt, a gantry, a solenoid bench and a brake
  arm. Guide: `docs/physics/actuators.md`.

## Lamps (RFC 0021) and fire (RFC 0022)

- **Lamps:** spot, point and beacon lamps (`LampAPI_1`) draw power from their bus and heat
  their node. Their light is drawn in the realistic look of the viewer and the cameras, and the
  light sensor reads it.
- **Fire:** `FireAPI_1` fires grow, radiate heat into robots, spread, and are put out by water or
  foam. Their smoke dims lidars, cameras and the visibility sensor.
- **Nozzles and tanks:** they spray along a real jet. The firefighting AMR sample puts out a
  pallet fire.

## Vegetation and trees

- **Grass, reeds and shrubs:** wheels roll on them, they drag on what moves through them, and
  lidars see their canopy. Propellers cut them. The realistic view draws grass as 3D blades.
- **Trees** (`TreeAPI_1`):
  - **Crowns and sway:** a crown is a volume of leaves that sways in the wind and the rotor wake.
  - **Cutting:** propellers cut through the crown and leaves fly off, settle, and blow up
    again in outwash.
  - **Branches:** the branches inside are solid. They stop a propeller, damaging the blade so it
    loses thrust and shakes the drone. They also stop a body.
- **Sensors and drawing:** lidars and cameras see crowns, branches and leaves. Trees are drawn
  in every shading.
- **Orchard sample:** `assets/worlds/orchard` has 140 trees and runs several times faster than
  real time on a CPU.

## Also

- **Roads:** OpenDRIVE objects and signals are imported, and the signals drive the traffic
  lights.
- **Radar:** echoes lose the two-way rain attenuation of ITU-R P.838-3.
- **Water:** rotor downwash moves the water's surface and raises spray.
- **Launching:** a desktop app entry is added. `lagrange` alone opens the launcher, and
  `lagrange build` opens the robot builder.
- **Fixes:** MuJoCo joint limits are now hard stops, and the Jolt backend keeps a swinging
  joint's energy.

## Upgrading from 1.4.x

- **Launching:** `lagrange` with nothing after it opens the viewer's launcher. The sample AMR
  in the warehouse is now `lagrange amr`.
- **MuJoCo state hashes:** MuJoCo runs whose joints reach their limits give new state hashes,
  because limits are stiff stops now.
- **Jolt state hashes:** Jolt runs give new state hashes, and a saved Jolt state carries each
  hinge's last arms.
- **The open field:** its trees are `TreeAPI_1` trees now (soft crowns over trunk colliders),
  so a drone flying into one is cut and slowed instead of bouncing off a sphere.
